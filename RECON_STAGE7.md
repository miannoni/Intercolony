## Source-plan summaries

### F25 — two sentences

F25 asks the player to post the work requirements, while the market returns candidates with their own wage offers, with quality and price correlated but still stochastic. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:643-676` It explicitly reuses skill, supply, distance, settlement profile, employer reputation, term, combat clause and scarcity rather than creating a disconnected labour-value model. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:678-691`

### F23 — two sentences

F23 asks for progressively capable supplied loadouts whose availability responds to the worker, settlement, tech, wealth and scarcity, rather than letting a poor source create endgame gear. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:784-802` It requires a separate refundable bond for issued equipment, meaningful loss/non-return consequences, and a much harsher economic and reputational response to deliberately removing valuable body modifications. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:804-842`

### F24 — two sentences

F24 asks for urgent labour requests with a worker count, combat regime, skill, short term, equipment level and emergency dispatch, drawing only from workers who actually exist, qualify and can reach the urgent window. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:852-882` It makes urgency a large scarcity, mobilisation and transport premium, with drop pods reserved for exceptional fast arrival rather than routine hiring. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:884-917`

### F22 — two sentences

F22 asks the player to list eligible colonists for external employment with a regime, term bounds and minimum compensation, then receive offers over time from settlements. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:701-746` It treats outside employment as the reciprocal labour economy, with abstract skill training and job-type-dependent risk turning income into a trade against absence and possible injury or death. `docs/PLAYTEST_BATCH_SOURCE_PLAN.md:748-780`

## D. What exists, and what is missing, for a genuinely two-sided market

### READ — the existing worker-side interests

There is already a worker reservation price: a prospect applies only when the player's posted wage clears the prospect's computed ask, and the applicant retains that ask for the player to inspect. `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPostingService.cs:156-176`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPostingService.cs:288-300`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPosting.cs:50-68` Applicants can also leave the queue after a patience period, workers can walk out after repeated missed payroll, and an active worker can decline renewal. `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPostingService.cs:419-457`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/PayrollService.cs:164-182`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/RenewalService.cs:59-101`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/RenewalService.cs:232-260`

The worker's conduct is not cosmetic: completion, missed payroll, arrears and walk-out feed the employer reputation that affects future labour. `Source/Intercolony/Reputation/EmployerReputationService.cs:103-151`; `Source/Intercolony/Reputation/EmployerReputation.cs:18-35`

### CONCLUDE — not yet reciprocal

The current posting side is still player-led: the UI asks for a wage, `JobPosting` persists `wageOffered`, the matcher compares that wage to a worker ask, and acceptance forms a contract at the posted wage. `Source/Intercolony/UI/Dialog_CreateJobPosting.cs:194-197`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPosting.cs:184-198`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPostingService.cs:163-176`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/EmploymentService.cs:255-304` That is a one-sided offer with a worker reservation-price filter, not two independent sides that can both quote, negotiate, accept or withdraw. `Source/Intercolony/Labor/JobPostingService.cs:163-176`; `Source/Intercolony/Labor/EmploymentService.cs:255-304`

The reverse side required by F22 is **NOT FOUND**: there is no player-colonist labour listing, no settlement offer queue, no external-employment record, and no source-specific offer decision in `Source/Intercolony`. The exact searches and their results are in §G. The vanilla lend-and-return quest part is a useful transport/ownership precedent, but it is not currently wired into Intercolony. `reference/decompiled/RimWorld/QuestPart_LendColonistsToFaction.cs:9-27`; exact source search in §G
